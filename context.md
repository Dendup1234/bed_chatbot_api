## What BetterEveryday is

**Tagline:** *Small Daily Habits. Extraordinary Lifelong Results.*

BetterEveryday is an AI-powered family wellness app that helps individuals and families build consistent daily habits across three pillars:

- **Fitness**
- **Diet**
- **Learning** (books, podcasts, and articles)

It combines quick logging tools (a timer, a photo, or your voice) with an **AI Coach** that scores your day and gives personal encouragement and suggestions. Progress is shared through streaks, badges, and family and friends leaderboards, so building habits becomes a shared, motivating experience.

**Core idea:** One small habit a day. One better choice. One day at a time. A check-in takes about **15 seconds**.

**Philosophy:** Built for consistency, not perfection. "When one person becomes better, the family grows stronger. And when families grow stronger, communities become better too."

**Who it's for:**
- Families with children who want to build healthy routines together
- Health-conscious adults who want to track their own growth

**Availability:** Coming soon to iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play). People can follow on social media to hear the moment it launches.

## Our story

BetterEveryday began as a hand-drawn **reflection chart on a wall at home**. Two children wrote down what they ate, how they moved, and what they read each day, with a column for big ideas too. There were no points and no app, just the habit of noticing small good choices together. The chart's three columns (Eating Healthy, Exercise, Reading) became the app's three pillars (Diet, Fitness, Learning), now with an AI Coach, family streaks, and shared progress.

## How it works (4 steps)

1. **Create:** Start your family team. Invite everyone with a code, a link, or a QR scan.
2. **Track:** Log your day in about 15 seconds with a timer, a photo, or your voice, across Fitness, Diet, and Learning.
3. **Improve:** Get your AI Coach score for the day, with encouragement and one next step.
4. **Grow together:** Build streaks as a family and watch the family streak wall climb.

## The three pillars

### Fitness
- **Live workout timer.** Tap Start, exercise, tap Finish. The timer keeps running even if you switch apps or lock your phone.
- **Multiple exercises per session** (for example a strength workout with several movements). Sets and reps can include weight.
- **GPS for outdoor activities** (runs, walks, cycles) tracks distance, pace, and route. If GPS is unavailable, logging still works with the timer only.
- **Workout types:** Run, Walk, Cycle, Strength, Yoga, or Custom, set as indoor or outdoor.
- **Repeat last workout in one tap.**
- **Routines:** build reusable workout templates (for example a "Chest Day") from an exercise library, with the option to add your own exercises. The app shows what you did last time for reference.
- **Forgot to start the timer?** You can log a workout afterwards.
- **History and stats:** list or calendar view, filters, weekly and monthly charts, and comparison with the previous period.

### Diet
- **Snap to log:** take a photo of your meal and AI estimates the food items, portions, calories, and macros. No manual entry and no searching a food database.
- **Quick corrections:** adjust with taps and sliders rather than typing. The AI learns from your corrections over time.
- **Say it instead:** describe what you ate and it gets logged. You always confirm before it is saved.
- **One-tap favourites:** meals you eat often appear automatically for one-tap logging.
- **Daily intake view:** calories against your goal with a macro breakdown, plus weekly and monthly trends.
- **Diet Stat Card:** a football-style hexagon card scoring things like Protein, Fiber, Hydration, Variety, Consistency, and Control, with tips for improving each. Sharing it is opt-in.
- **Fasting timer:** one-tap fasting with presets such as 16:8 and 18:6. Informational only, not medical advice. Reminders are optional and off by default.
- **Goal-linked nudges:** optional, time-aware notifications when you are falling behind a plan target. Off by default and fully configurable.
- **Kind to your habits:** rewards are tied to consistent logging and hitting goals, not just eating less. Streaks include forgiveness so one missed day doesn't wipe your progress.

### Learning
- **One timer and one library** for **books, podcasts, and articles**.
- **Learning timer:** tap Start, read or listen, tap Stop. It keeps running in the background.
- **Add items easily:** scan a book barcode or search; paste a link for podcasts and articles; or type details in by hand.
- **After each session:** optionally add pages read (books) or mark as finished/read (podcasts, articles), plus a short **learning note** (up to 500 characters) about what you learned or enjoyed.
- **The learning note matters:** the AI Coach scores Learning mainly from the note. If no note is written that day, Learning scores 0 even if time was logged.
- **Book progress bar** if you enter the total page count.
- **Voice or typed logging** for sessions you forgot to time (for example "Read 30 pages of Atomic Habits this morning"). You confirm before it saves.
- **History and stats:** filters, search, streaks, and charts of time per day and week.

## AI Coach and Sprout

- The **AI Coach** gives your day a score (0-100) based on what you actually logged across Fitness, Diet, and Learning, with personal encouragement and suggestions.
- Example tone: "Great walk today. A short read tonight will round it off."
- **Sprout** is the friendly mascot who greets you during onboarding and reacts to your choices. Sprout is being developed as the friendly face of the app's AI guidance, based on your own logged data. Don't claim more detail than this.
- Onboarding with Sprout lets you pick habits to focus on (such as Move more, Eat well, Read daily, Drink water, Stay calm, Sleep better) and choose a daily check-in time.

## Family, friends, and fun

- **Family groups:** create a family, invite members by code, link, or QR code (invite codes expire after 7 days or on first use). You can belong to **more than one family** (for example your household and your parents' family). Roles are per family: Admin or Member.
- **Children:** child accounts come with **parental controls**, including an approval step when a child joins. Parents can see their children's logs.
- **Family Missions:** one shared mission a day keeps everyone pointed at the same goal.
- **Family streak wall:** see everyone's streak status.
- **Family Highlight Story:** a weekly AI-generated recap of the family's activity, shareable on WhatsApp or Facebook.
- **Friends:** add friends outside your family by username or friend code (both people must approve). Friends have their own leaderboard, kept **separate** from the family streak wall.
- **Leaderboards:** daily, weekly, and monthly rankings (reading time, pages read, workout duration or distance, healthy meals logged). Opt in or out per leaderboard. **Competing is never on by default.**
- **Streaks and badges:** daily and weekly streaks with fire icons, points, achievements (for example First Book, 10 Workouts, 30-Day Streak).
- **Growing avatar:** an avatar that levels up as points and badges grow. Especially motivating for kids.
- **Challenges:** fun challenges can pop up so the family or friend group stays productive together.
- **Notifications:** configurable reminders to log, protect a streak, or finish a timer. Parents can receive family activity alerts.
